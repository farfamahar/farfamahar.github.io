*September.8/2025*
<br>
- Wow it's been a year since I started this micro dev log. Today I made tons of progress. Most productive I've been in a while. Removed a lot of placeholder visuals. Fine tuned transitions and refined the desert scene with an incredible camera script. Experimented with some forest visuals and changed all cameras to different aspect ratios. Also a lot of post processing visual experiments.
- Something I'm realizing is that I work best with no plan. The design document was holding me back. Everything great I've made has been me entering the editor with a spark of an idea. THe flames come from playing around, breaking stuff, combining things in strange ways. This is how I enter a creative flow state. The design document at this point is just a visual dump of ideas to potentially explore. Some sparks just in case I need some. But I really don't need any of that. Art is about exploring a new reality based on the trillions of variables in my mind.
