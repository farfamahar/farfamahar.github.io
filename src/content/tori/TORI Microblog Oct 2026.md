*October.18/2026*
<br>
- Went to an event called Estatic Dance. Seemed like a good event to encourage moving my body in extreme ways. The event has a spirtual tone to it mixed with the freedom and fun of body movement via dance. The dancing was something I got a lot of it. The event encourages movement in a judgement free way. Its a somatic experience. Jumping, hopping, twisting, shaking, freezing, falling. Things that are hard to do in clubs or raves - it can be done here. Which was great as field research for the final sequence in TORI as well as an incredible workout.
